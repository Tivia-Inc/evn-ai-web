Questions

Do I need a big following?

No. We look at how your Reels do with your own audience, not just the follower count. 5K–100K is our sweet spot.

I’m not in the US. Can I apply?

Not this round. We’re working with US-based creators who are 21 or older.

Do I have to pay for the app?

No. We send you a link for free Pro before you film.

What is glucose load?

It’s a way to look at the carb impact of a meal, based on both the type and amount of carbs, not just calories. Evn AI shows it, with calories, from a photo. It’s an estimate for curiosity and better swaps, not a medical test.

What can’t I say?

Please don’t promise results (skin, weight, energy) or talk to viewers about their health. Say what the app shows: “this one came out lower.” The brief has a short say/don’t-say list.

Is there a script?

No. We send a one-page brief and a few hooks that have worked. The words are yours, and no nitpicky edits as long as it follows the brief.

Can I post on TikTok too?

Yes. Reels is the main post; the view bonus counts whichever is highest of Reels, TikTok or Shorts.

Will you use my video in ads?

Only if we ask and you say yes. We pay +$30 for 60 days of use.

Is the woman in these videos real?

No. Chloe is an AI character we use on our own channels. That’s why we need you.
