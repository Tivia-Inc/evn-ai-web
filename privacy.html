2. 음식 사진 처리 과정

  1. 기기에서 사진을 긴 변 1024px로 줄이고, 위치 정보를 포함한 EXIF를 지웁니다.
  2. 사진을 암호화 통신(HTTPS)으로 회사의 서버 함수에 보냅니다. 서버 함수는 구독과 이용 한도를 확인한 뒤 사진을 Google Gemini API(유료 티어)와 Anthropic Claude API 중 한 곳으로 넘기고, 그곳이 오류를 내거나 제때 응답하지 않으면 같은 사진을 다른 곳으로도 넘깁니다. 어느 곳에 먼저 넘길지는 회사가 인식 품질과 안정성을 보고 서버에서 정합니다. 이전 버전 앱(1.0.0)은 Gemini API만 씁니다. 익명 ID 등 이용자를 알아볼 수 있는 정보는 Google과 Anthropic에 보내지 않습니다.

5. Retention and deletion

"Delete all my data" in the Profile tab deletes the photos, scan history, body info, weight log, goals and settings on your device, cancels scheduled meal reminders, and deletes your anonymous account on our server. The app keeps the choice you made with "Share usage data" on that device (off, or on where it starts off), so deleting your data doesn't change it. Deleting the account also deletes your sign-in session records, daily scan counts, free-scan record, support requests and recognition reports. The next time you open the app, it creates a new anonymous ID.

It does not delete:

  • Your subscription. Cancel it separately in your app store's subscription settings (see our Support page).
  • RevenueCat's subscription records, which are kept to manage your subscription, restore purchases and handle refunds. To have them deleted, ask us (section 7) and we'll delete them for you.
  • Usage events in PostHog, and the approximate location kept with your anonymous ID. Ask us and we'll delete them (section 7).
  • Server logs, which stay for as long as Supabase keeps them (section 1).
  • Emails you send to support@evnai.app. Ask us and we'll delete them.

You need to be online. If the app can't reach our server, it deletes nothing, not even on your device, and tells you, so you can try again when you're online.

If you only delete the app, the data on your device goes with it, but your anonymous account records on our server (sign-in session records, free-scan record, support requests, recognition reports) remain. Tap "Delete all my data" before deleting the app. We don't automatically delete inactive anonymous accounts.

Device backups: photos, history, body info and weight entries stored on your device may be included in your device's cloud backup (such as iCloud), which keeps your history when you move to a new device. Deleting data in the app doesn't remove copies already in those backups; manage them in your device's backup settings. We can't access these backups.
