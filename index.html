before you download

Got questions?

Is Evn AI a diet app?

Nope. There are no food rules and nothing's off-limits. You can set a daily goal if you like having one, or skip it.

How accurate is it?

It's an estimate, and no food app gets it right every time. You'll see every food it found, so if something's off, fix it or add what's missing in a tap.

I paid, but I'm still seeing the paywall. Help?

Sorry, that's annoying. Open the paywall and tap "Restore purchases." Just make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

You cancel in your phone's settings, not in the app. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Evn AI. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Heads up: deleting the app doesn't cancel your subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle refunds, so the request goes to them. On iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, go through Google Play.

Is this medical advice?

No. Evn AI gives you estimates so you can get to know your food better. It can't diagnose or treat anything. If you have diabetes or another health condition, check with your doctor.

What happens to my photos?

Each photo is sent to an AI service (Google or Anthropic, in the US) so the food can be recognized. Evn AI doesn't store it on its servers. You can delete your history and your account anytime in the app's settings.

Is it on Android?

Almost! Evn AI is on the App Store now, and Google Play is coming soon.

Is it for me?

Probably, if you love your carbs (sweets included), have zero plans to quit them, and prefer easy habits to strict plans. Probably not, if you need medical advice or want every gram tracked.
