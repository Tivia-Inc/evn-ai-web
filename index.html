before you hit download

Got a question?

Is Evn AI a diet app?

No. There are no food rules and nothing is off-limits. Daily goals are optional and just a starting point.

How accurate is it?

It's an estimate. No food app is perfect. You can see every food it found, and fix or add one in a tap.

I paid but the app still shows the paywall. What gives?

Annoying, but fixable. Open the paywall and tap "Restore purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

You can't cancel from inside the app. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Evn AI. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Heads up: deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play.

Is this medical advice?

No. Evn AI is for curiosity, not a diagnosis or treatment. If you have diabetes or another condition, talk to your doctor.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo goes to an AI service (Google or Anthropic, USA) for recognition and isn't stored on Evn AI's servers. You can delete your history and account anytime in the app's settings.

Is it on Android?

Evn AI is on the App Store now. Google Play is coming soon.

Who is it for?

For you if you love bread and don't want to quit it, and like soft routines more than strict plans. Maybe not for you if you need medical advice or want every gram weighed.
